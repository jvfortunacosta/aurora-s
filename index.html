Frequently Asked Questions

How is the cleaning price determined?

The price is calculated after we assess your home, taking into account factors like the size of the house, the desired frequency (weekly, biweekly, or monthly), and whether you have pets, since these details affect the service.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No, it's not necessary. We actually prefer that clients not be home during the cleaning, so the work can be done more efficiently.

Do I need to be present at any point?

Yes, only for the initial assessment when we provide your quote. That's when we evaluate the space and discuss frequency and any special details (like pets) to finalize the price.

Does the cleaning frequency affect the price?

Yes. We offer weekly, biweekly, and monthly options, and the price varies depending on the frequency you choose.
